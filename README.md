## Hi there 👋 

I mainly use my GitHub account to host some of the more refined projects and scientific work from my computer science studies. My latest projects include:
- A SoK [paper](https://github.com/LunaNordin/Mechanistic-Interpretability-of-Large-Language-Models) giving an overview of mechanistic interpretability techniques for LLMs. It describes and compares the basic interpretability approaches of circuit discovery through activation patching and monosemantic feature learning with sparse autoencoders.
- A SoK [paper](https://github.com/LunaNordin/Prompt-Injection-Attacks) outlining the mechanism behind prompt injection attacks on LLMs. It summarizes multiple manual and automated methods for attack prompt optimization, discusses the influence of instruction-following fine-tuning on attack success rates, and outlines some basic defense approaches.
- [Campus-Plan-Bot](https://github.com/thomasmarwitz/campus_plan_bot): a user-friendly chatbot that helps people navigate the KIT campus. It uses a RAG-based natural language interface and was developed in collaboration with [@thomasmarwitz](https://github.com/thomasmarwitz).
- A seminar [essay](https://github.com/LunaNordin/AGI-Benchmarks-Feasability-Implications) and presentation on the problems and complexities of defining AGI. It goes into detail on why AGI definitions fail and how this impacts benchmarking methods, prediction of development timelines and impacts, as well as approaches to reach AGI.
- A [quantitative analysis](https://github.com/LunaNordin/Efficient-Probabilistic-Inference-with-Orthogonal-Tensor-Train-Decomposition) of inference in hidden Markov models that are represented through tensor networks.
